Internal · Reference · Live-ops A

Same shop, different plug. Brand does not change the booking.

Non-DJI operators never use DJI Cloud API. They finish jobs in Stravyx with status taps and manual upload. Supported DJI operators may also plug Pilot 2 into Stravyx — same booking, same customer downloads.

Audience Founders, operators, product

Authority ADR 0005 · architecture Story C
01What is the same for everyone
Stravyx is the marketplace. The customer never needs a DJI or Autel login. Connecting a DJI remote is never required to tap Accept.
  1. Customer books and pays one Network Price.
  2. Eligible licensed companies (ReOC) see the offer.
  3. First to Accept wins; others are locked out. Full street address appears after Accept (suburb only before).
  4. Operator flies the site.
  5. Files land in Stravyx, sit held, then the operator Releases them.
  6. Customer downloads from Stravyx — not from DJI, Autel, or email.
02What is different (the plug, not the shop)
Non-DJI (Autel, Skydio, others) Supported DJI (Pilot 2 / Dock, after privacy)
Fly in Their own flying app DJI Pilot 2 on the handheld remote
DJI Cloud API Never Optional hose: remote talks to our cloud
Progress in Stravyx Operator taps Ready → Airborne → Complete Taps can be filled from live radio; customer may get a coarse map pin if approved
Files into Stravyx Copy off card/drone, upload in the website for this job Pilot 2 can auto-upload, or they can still upload by hand
If radio / privacy off This is their path They fall back to the same taps + upload
DJI Cloud API is DJI’s protocol where Pilot 2 (or a Dock) talks to Stravyx-hosted servers — not FlightHub 2. Autel/Skydio cannot speak it. A later Autel connector would be their API, not DJI’s. Manual is the spare tyre: non-DJI, unsupported DJI, APP 8 still open, or radio down.

04User stories
Priya is the customer. Brooke, Alex, Chris, and Dana are operators. Aircraft brand must not change who can win or what Priya pays.
Story 1 · Autel only

Brooke never opens DJI software

Priya books a roof inspection. Brooke’s Autel-only company taps Accept first. She flies in Autel’s app, taps Ready → Airborne → Complete in Stravyx, copies photos to a laptop, and Uploads for this job. Files are held. She presses Release. Priya downloads from Stravyx. DJI Cloud API is not involved.

Story 2 · Supported DJI, live radio allowed

Alex plugs Pilot 2 into his company’s Stravyx room

Same booking. Alex wins. His remote is already in his company’s room. He assigns aircraft SN-123, flies in Pilot 2. Priya may see a pin. Photos can auto-land in our cupboard. He Releases. Priya still downloads from Stravyx, not FlightHub. If the radio dies, he finishes like Brooke. The job must not be stuck.

Story 3 · APP 8 still open

Owning DJI hardware does not force Cloud API

Live radio is off in production. Alex flies in Pilot 2 locally (screen on the remote, files on the card) and completes exactly like Brooke.

Story 4 · Mixed fleet

Chris has a Matrice 30 and an Autel

Matrice job (after privacy): he may use Cloud API. Autel job: always manual for that aircraft. Accept is one tap either way. He does not need the Matrice online to take an Autel job.

Story 5 · Unsupported DJI

Dana’s Mini is manual, same as Autel

A consumer DJI Mini is not on our Cloud API hardware list. We do not tell her to “use Cloud API anyway.”

Story 6 · First-to-accept fairness

Priya does not pick a brand

She sees one Network Price, not “DJI vs Autel.” We must not rank who gets the job by whose DJI is online. Winner might be Brooke (manual) or Alex (live). Priya still gets raw photos from Stravyx after Release.

Story 7 · Radio outage

Already accepted, then the controller cannot reach us

Alex still taps Complete and uploads. Same Release. Same customer download.

Story 8 · Later, not Live-ops A

An Autel connector would be Autel’s API, not DJI’s

If we add it, Brooke might one day get auto-upload via their cloud. Maps/3D (Layer 2) stay a Stravyx processing product — Brooke is not sent to FlightHub.

05What we will not do

Locked product rules

  • Require DJI Cloud API (or any DJI account) to Accept or to be paid.
  • Make customers log into a drone-maker website.
  • Leave a job stranded because live radio failed.
  • Rank dispatch by device-online.
  • Pretend manual Track Job is a live map (status labels only, unless a later product/privacy decision adds something like phone GPS).
