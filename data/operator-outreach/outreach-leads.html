How to use the files

Primary (Founding Operators)

02 → 03 → 04 → 06. Only invite licence_type=reoc. Pitch: pre-built profile, reduced commission, priority dispatch.

Secondary (verify first)

05 has 630 metro listings with mobiles but ARN/RePL IDs — confirm they hold their own ReOC before Founding Operator invite (excluded-category pilots are post-MVP).

CASA public register confirms ~3,267 active ReOC certificates nationally; directory covers ~28% with contactable ReOC businesses. Gap (~2,300) needs company-name enrichment (ABN/website) before cold outreach — do not blast CASA-only rows without a published contact path.
