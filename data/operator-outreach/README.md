## Sources

- **Primary contacts:** [Drones For Hire](https://dronesforhire.com.au/drone-operator-directory) public listings (operators self-published mobiles for hire).
- **Licence universe:** [CASA ReOC holders](https://www.casa.gov.au/drones/remotely-piloted-aircraft-operators-certificate/remotely-piloted-aircraft-operators-certificate-reoc-holders) (~3,267 certificates).

## Privacy

Treat phone numbers as personal information. Prefer operators who published contacts for commercial work. Complete the Privacy Act / APP review before bulk outreach from the CASA register alone. Do not push these CSVs to a public remote without redaction.
